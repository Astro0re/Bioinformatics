# ABO Gene

## Introduction

This analysis is to take a deep dive into the features and properties of
the Blood type determining gene factor the ABO Gene, Using
Bioinformatics tools to compare and analyse this gene, looking into it's
sequencial and structural make up, as well as looking at possible
mutations and it's effects.

Gene sequence gotten from the [National Center of Biotechnology
information (NCBI)](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/)

## Sequence

General information about the sequence ie location, size 
The ABO gene discoverd about 100 years ago, has been of great importance to the
understanding and application of inherited characteristic and potential
immunogenic impact during transfusions(Dean, 2005). 
Located on the 9th chromosome and a lenght of 24801
